---
title: How to Use Codex
description: Use Codex as a learning-system maintainer, not an answer machine.
---

Codex should support the learning system. It should not replace your proof work.

## Use Codex As

### Curriculum Generator

Ask Codex to create structured weeks and days from the curriculum schema.

### Proof Feedback Assistant

Paste your attempted proof and ask Codex to identify missing assumptions, invalid implications, weak induction hypotheses, or skipped cases.

### Exercise Generator

Ask for additional problems by topic and difficulty after you have attempted the original set.

### Lab Builder

Ask for small Python labs that test intuition. Require `assert` tests and a statement that the lab is not proof.

### Review Pack Builder

Ask Codex to generate flashcards, repair drills, and retrospectives from your actual mistakes.

### Source Auditor

Ask Codex to check whether resources are official, open, and legal. Never substitute unauthorized mirrors.

## Do Not Use Codex As

- a shortcut to avoid writing proofs;
- a source of uncited textbook summaries;
- a way to skip definitions;
- an authority for legal source access;
- a generator of large unreviewed content dumps.

## Prompt Pattern

```text
Read context/current_context.md and AGENTS.md.
Use prompts/{prompt_name}.md.
Here is my attempt or current state:
...
Generate targeted feedback and update context/state.
```
