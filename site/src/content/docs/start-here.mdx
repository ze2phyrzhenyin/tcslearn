This page is the operational entry point. If you have not studied Week 1 yet, start with Day 1.

## Today

1. Open [Week 1 Day 1](/week01/day01/).
2. Read the goals and prerequisites.
3. Copy the definitions by hand.
4. Work through the minimal examples without looking back.
5. Attempt the Day 1 exercise sheet before reading any solution.
6. Record mistakes in `state/mistakes_log.md`.
7. Use the next Codex prompt only after you have written your own attempt.

## How to Use Notes

Read notes as working documents, not as blog posts. For every definition, produce:

- the formal statement;
- one example;
- one non-example;
- one sentence explaining why the non-example fails.

For theorem or claim sections, separate assumptions, goal, strategy, proof, and step check.

## How to Do Exercises

Use the exercise pages as the primary work surface. Do not read solutions first. For proof exercises:

1. Restate the claim.
2. List definitions used.
3. Write assumptions.
4. Choose a strategy.
5. Complete the proof.
6. Check each nontrivial step.

## How to Read Solutions

Solutions are for diagnosis. Compare your proof with the solution and classify the gap:

- missing definition;
- wrong quantifier;
- invalid implication;
- weak induction hypothesis;
- missing sample space;
- wrong Big-O direction;
- reduction direction reversed.

## How to Record Mistakes

After each session, update `state/mistakes_log.md` with the exact error and a repair exercise. Use [Mistakes to Watch](/review/mistakes-to-watch/) as the category map.

## How to Ask Codex for Feedback

After studying Day 1 or taking the Week 1 self-test, copy [Next Actions](/meta/next-actions/) or `context/next_codex_prompt.md`. Paste your own failed attempts and ask for diagnostic feedback, not just answers.
