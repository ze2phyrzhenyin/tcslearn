\section*{Instructions}
For each counting problem, state whether objects are ordered and whether repetition is allowed.

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{D4.\arabic*.}]
\item Count length-5 strings over alphabet $\{0,1,2\}$.
\item Count length-5 strings over $\{0,1,2\}$ with no repeated symbol adjacent.
\item Count 3-element subsets of a 10-element set.
\item Count permutations of 6 distinct objects.
\item Count integers in $\{1,\dots,100\}$ divisible by 2 or 5 using inclusion-exclusion.
\item Count binary strings of length 8 with at least one 1 using complement.
\item Use pigeonhole to prove that among 13 people, two have birth month in common.
\item Use pigeonhole to prove that any 6 integers contain two whose difference is divisible by 5.
\item Prove every finite tree with $n$ vertices has $n-1$ edges.
\item Prove every connected graph with $n$ vertices has at least $n-1$ edges.
\item Determine whether a cycle of odd length is bipartite.
\item Model binary strings of length at most 3 as a rooted tree.
\item Model a simple DFA as a directed graph.
\item Model dependencies among tasks as a directed graph and explain what a cycle means.
\end{enumerate}
